Door 1 · For owners

Your building, handled. Your money, accounted for.

Most of our owners have one to three buildings and a full-time job. We take the phone calls, the turnovers and the 2 a.m. leaks, and you get one clear statement a month.

What we handle

  • Leasing & showings

    Listing, photos, showings, one written screening standard for every applicant, lease signing.

  • Rent collection

    Online payments, auto-pay, deposits tracked to the penny and held the way state law requires.

  • Repairs, 24/7

    Local licensed trades at cost. Anything over your limit waits for your OK, except true emergencies.

  • Inspections & code

    Move-in and move-out photo reports, smoke and CO certificates, city inspections scheduled for you.

  • Monthly statements

    Income, every expense with its invoice, and your deposit, by the 10th of each month.

    You get a named manager with a direct line, not a ticket number or a call center.

FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

Don't see yours? Call the office, Monday to Friday, 9 to 5.

(413) 555-0158
What kinds of buildings do you manage?

Mostly 2–6 family homes and small apartment buildings up to about 24 units, in Holyoke, Chicopee, South Hadley, Easthampton and Springfield. If yours is a little outside that, ask.

When and how do I get paid?

By direct deposit on the 10th of each month, with a statement that lists rent collected, every expense with its invoice, and our fee.

Do I get a say in repairs?

Yes. You set a dollar limit. Anything above it waits for your OK. True emergencies, like no heat in winter or an active leak, get handled right away and you hear from us the same day.

How do you choose tenants?

Every applicant is reviewed with the same written criteria, applied the same way, in line with federal and Massachusetts fair housing law. We share those criteria with you before we list your unit.

How do tenants reach maintenance after hours?

The maintenance line, (413) 555-0158 then press 2, is answered 24/7 for emergencies. Everything else goes through the repair request form or the resident portal.

Can I leave if it isn't working out?

Yes. Give 30 days' written notice. There's no cancellation fee, and we hand over leases, deposits, keys and records in an orderly way.

Thinking about handing off a building?

Start with a free rent estimate, or call and talk it through with Dana. No pressure, no long contract.
