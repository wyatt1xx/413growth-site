Services

Everyday care, done carefully.

We keep things simple: the visits most pets need, done in-house by the same two vets, with a written estimate before anything beyond the exam.

Wellness exams

A nose-to-tail check once a year, more often for puppies and kittens. Time for your questions is built in.

  • 30-minute appointments
  • Weight & nutrition check-ins

Vaccines

Core and lifestyle vaccines on a schedule that fits your pet, with reminders by text or email.

  • Rabies certificates same day
  • Boarding paperwork

Dental care

Cleanings under anesthesia with full monitoring, plus at-home tips that actually stick.

  • Dental X-rays in-house
  • February dental month

Surgery

Spays, neuters, and routine soft-tissue procedures, with a clear estimate and a call from the surgeon when your pet wakes up.

  • Pain plan for every patient
  • Free recheck at 10–14 days

Senior care

Twice-yearly visits for pets seven and up, with bloodwork and a gentler pace. Quiet rooms, non-slip mats, no rushing.

  • Comfort-focused exams
  • Mobility & diet guidance

What to bring

  • Past records

    Vaccine history and any recent lab work from your previous vet.

  • List of medications

    Names and doses of anything your pet takes, including supplements and flea or tick products.

  • Carrier or leash

    Cats in a carrier, dogs on a short leash. A favorite blanket helps.

  • New-client forms

    We'll email them after you book. Fill them out at home to skip the clipboard.

Questions

Good to know.

Can't find your answer? Call the front desk. A real person picks up during open hours.

Book a visit
Are you taking new patients?

Yes. We're currently accepting new dogs and cats. Most new-patient visits are booked within one to two weeks.

Do you handle emergencies?

No. We're a scheduled-care clinic. For emergencies after hours, call your nearest 24-hour animal hospital. During open hours, call us first and we'll tell you whether to come in or go straight there.

Do you see animals other than dogs and cats?

Not right now. Keeping to dogs and cats lets our two vets stay sharp on what they see every day.

Will I know the cost before treatment?

Always. You'll get a written estimate before anything beyond the exam, and we'll call before going over it.

How do I get my pet's records transferred?

Give us your previous clinic's name when you book, and we'll request the records for you.

Can I stay with my pet during the exam?

Yes, for exams and vaccines. For dental work and surgery, we'll send updates and call when it's time for pickup.
