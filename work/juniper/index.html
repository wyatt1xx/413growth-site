About the studio

About Studio Juniper

We opened Studio Juniper because getting your hair done and getting stronger should both feel like a breath out.

The salon has four chairs by tall windows; the Pilates room next door has four reformers and space for six on the mat. We book enough time to do the work properly, use low-fragrance products, and keep the music quiet enough to talk or not talk.
