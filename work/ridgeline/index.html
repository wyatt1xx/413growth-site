Questions

Good to know before you call.

Do you really call back within the hour?

Usually, yes, on weekdays between 7 AM and 6 PM. On the busiest cold mornings it can slip; when it does, we text to let you know where you are in line instead of leaving you guessing.

What counts as an emergency after hours?

No heat, a water heater or pipe actively leaking, or a CO alarm (after you've left the house and called 911). Everything else we'll book first thing the next business day.

Is a heat pump worth it in an old New England house?

Often, especially if you're on oil or electric baseboard. Cold-climate models run well below zero. We'll look at your insulation and current system and give you an honest answer, including when keeping your boiler as backup makes sense.

Can you help with Mass Save rebates?

Yes. We'll tell you which programs your project likely qualifies for and help with the paperwork. Rebate amounts change, so we don't quote a number until we know your setup.

Do you charge for estimates?

Replacement and heat-pump estimates are free. Repair visits have a flat diagnostic fee that we tell you on the phone, and it's applied to the repair if you go ahead.
