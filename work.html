Our work

Sites that answer the question before the phone rings.

Every site I build is fast on a phone, puts Call and Text one tap away, and says the things customers check before they pick a shop. Here are five, one for each kind of business I work with.

413 Growth is new, so these are sample sites for made-up businesses. Each one opens like a real site: try it on your phone.

ridgelineheating.com

HVAC and plumbing · Westfield

Ridgeline Heating & Cooling

A family heating shop that works the hill towns. The site gets a no-heat caller to a person in one tap.

  • License numbers on every page. Their HIC and plumbing license in the footer, not just “fully licensed”.
  • A card for each town they cover. Each with a real job done there, so they show up when people search their own town.
  • Call and Text always on top. A sticky bar on phones: one tap from anywhere on the page.
  • An honest callback time. “We usually call back within the hour, 7 AM to 6 PM.” Only if it’s true.

studiojuniper.com

Salon and Pilates · Northampton

Studio Juniper

A small salon with a Pilates room upstairs. The site answers “how much and how long” before anyone has to ask.

  • Prices on the homepage. “From” prices with how long each service takes.
  • A first-visit offer with an end date. One simple offer, clear terms. Never tied to a review.
  • A Book button that goes straight to booking. Into their booking app, not a contact form.
  • Reviews right on the page. Shown where people decide, not only a link out to Google.

maplerowvet.com

Vet clinic · Belchertown

Maple Row Animal Clinic

A two-vet country clinic. The site settles the three questions pet owners call about: are you taking us, when, and what if it’s urgent.

  • “Accepting new patients” up top. Said plainly, or a waitlist note.
  • One Book button everywhere. The same button on every section, straight to booking.
  • Holiday hours kept current. On the site and on Google before every holiday.
  • A plain “not for emergencies” note. Where to go after hours, plus what to bring to a first visit.

hillrowelaw.com

Estate and elder law · Longmeadow

Hill & Rowe Law

A two-lawyer estate planning firm. Plain English for families, and the notices Massachusetts requires, done right.

  • One plain section per practice area. Written for the client, not for other lawyers.
  • Free consult and Call on every section. With office hours, so nobody has to hunt.
  • The courts they’re in. A short list, so clients know they work nearby.
  • The required notices. Attorney advertising where it’s needed, and never “specialist”.

canalstreetpm.com

Property management · Holyoke

Canal Street Property Co.

A small property manager in the mill city. Owners and tenants each get their own front door.

  • Two front doors. One for owners, one for tenants, right at the top.
  • A dated fee page. What they charge and when it was last updated. Owners compare.
  • A free rent estimate form. The way the national companies reach owners, done locally.
  • Fair housing on every page. The company name and the Equal Housing Opportunity line, everywhere.
